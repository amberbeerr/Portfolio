Human Space
從 0→1 探索自我覺察的數位平台Building a self-awareness platform from 0→1
創辦人Founder
背景Context
2025 年 7 月自主啟動的個人專案，希望將過去十年的品牌與內容經驗，實驗性地應用在腦神經科學與心理學主題上，測試「大人的童書」這個定位能否被更廣泛的讀者接受。A self-initiated project launched in July 2025, applying a decade of brand and content experience to neuroscience and psychology topics — testing whether a "picture books for grown-ups" positioning could resonate beyond a niche wellness audience.
機會點Opportunity
中文市場的心理學／腦科學內容，往往偏學術或過度簡化。我看到中間存在一個機會：在不失準確性的前提下，把知識轉譯成好讀、好互動的內容形式。Psychology and neuroscience content in Chinese-language media tends to be either overly academic or oversimplified. I saw an opportunity to translate research into accessible, engaging formats without losing credibility.
我的角色My Role
獨立創辦人——負責定位、內容方向、社群成長，並自主學習 AI 輔助開發，獨力打造三款互動小工具。Independent founder — responsible for positioning, content direction and community growth, and self-taught AI-assisted development to independently build three interactive tools.
做法Approach
以「大人的童書」作為核心定位，將心理學概念拆解為輕量、可互動的內容單元，並用 AI 輔助工具快速將想法原型化、發布上線，不依賴技術團隊。Defined "picture books for grown-ups" as the core positioning; broke psychology concepts into lightweight, interactive content units; used AI-assisted tools to quickly prototype and publish experiments without a technical team.
關鍵決策Key Decisions
選擇多個小而聚焦的互動實驗，而非一開始就打造單一大型平台，讓每個想法能獨立驗證、快速迭代。Chose several small, focused interactive experiments over building one large platform upfront, so each idea could be validated and iterated independently.
執行Execution
上線後陸續推出三個互動實驗——感情 36 問、HSP 自我認識測驗、對話式短影音生成工具，皆在帳號成立後數週內獨立完成並發布。Launched three interactive experiments in succession — a relationship question deck, an HSP self-assessment quiz, and a dialogue-to-video tool — each independently built and shipped within weeks of the account's launch.

NourNow
以科學、內容與社群，建立健康與 Wellness 知識平台Building a health and wellness knowledge platform through science, content and community
共同創辦人 · 內容策略Co-Founder · Content Strategy
背景Context
2025 年 5 月共同創辦，7 月正式營運的華語健康媒體社群，目標是讓年輕華語讀者更容易理解並實踐健康、Wellness 相關知識。Co-founded in May 2025 and officially launched in July, a Chinese-language wellness media community aimed at making health and wellness knowledge more accessible for younger Chinese-speaking audiences.
機會點Opportunity
這個地區的健康／Wellness 內容常常過於臨床，或過度追逐流行話題；我們看到打造一個「有編輯品味、以社群為核心」的品牌的空間。Health and wellness content in the region tends to be either overly clinical or trend-chasing. We saw the opportunity to build an editorially credible, community-first brand instead.
我的角色My Role
共同創辦人，主責內容策略、編輯方向、品牌合作與社群活動規劃。Co-Founder, responsible for content strategy, editorial direction, brand partnerships and community event planning.
做法Approach
建立涵蓋營養科學、運動健康、Longevity、腦神經科學、心理健康等主題的內容支柱；透過與相關品牌合作建立可信度與觸及，而非宣稱自身具備臨床專業。Built content pillars spanning nutrition, fitness, longevity, neuroscience and mental wellbeing. Partnered with relevant brands to build credibility and reach, rather than claiming clinical expertise.
關鍵決策Key Decisions
除了數位內容，同步投入實體社群活動（10+ 場百人運動派對），優先建立信任與黏著度，而不只是追求觸及數字。Invested in in-person community events (10+ fitness gatherings with 100+ attendees) alongside digital content, prioritizing trust and retention over reach alone.
執行Execution
六個月內將 Instagram 粉絲成長至 23,000+、社群會員突破 2,000 人；建立與 Garmin、Columbia、綠藤生機、ESEN 等品牌的合作關係，並與 CouchSpace、Sports Nation、Chill Taiwan 共同策劃社群活動。Grew Instagram to 23,000+ followers and 2,000+ community members within six months. Built partnerships with Garmin, Columbia, Greenvines, ESEN and others, and co-hosted community events with CouchSpace, Sports Nation, and Chill Taiwan.

AI 輔助與數位實驗AI-assisted & Digital Experiments

在 Human Space 的實驗過程中，我自主學習並運用 AI 輔助工具，獨立將想法原型化並發布上線。以下是三個自發性的小型實驗，目的不是證明我是 AI 專家，而是展現我對新興工具的好奇心，以及把想法變成使用者可體驗介面的能力。As part of the Human Space experiment, I taught myself to use AI-assisted tools to independently prototype and publish ideas. The three self-initiated projects below aren't meant to prove technical expertise — they demonstrate curiosity, self-directed learning, and the ability to turn ideas into simple, user-facing experiences.

感情 36 問36 Questions
發想Idea
參考關係心理學研究，設計 36 道漸進式提問，幫助兩人建立更深的連結。A 36-question card deck, inspired by relationship psychology research, designed to build closeness between two people.
使用者需求User Need
人們想要有結構地建立關係連結，卻不知從何開始。People want a structured way to build meaningful connection but don't know where to start.
體驗Experience
簡單、手機友善的互動問題卡，可獨自使用或與他人一起進行。A simple, mobile-friendly interactive question deck, usable solo or with a partner.
AI 輔助Assistance
獨立自學使用 AI 輔助開發工具完成，無正式工程背景。Independently built using AI-assisted development tools, self-taught with no formal engineering background.
學到什麼What I Learned
一個範疇小、聚焦明確的互動體驗，也能不靠大團隊帶來實際的參與度與成長。A small, well-scoped interactive experience can drive meaningful engagement and growth without a large team.
HSP 自我認識測驗HSP Quiz
發想Idea
參考高敏感特質（Sensory Processing Sensitivity）相關研究設計的自我探索測驗，並非臨床診斷工具。An accessible self-assessment inspired by research on sensory processing sensitivity (HSP) — not a clinical diagnostic tool.
使用者需求User Need
對高敏感特質好奇的人，需要一個輕鬆、非臨床的方式認識自己。People curious about high-sensitivity traits want an easy, non-clinical way to reflect on their own tendencies.
體驗Experience
將心理學概念轉譯為生活化語言的簡短互動測驗。A short interactive quiz that translates psychological concepts into everyday language.
AI 輔助Assistance
以 AI 輔助工具開發，內容由心理學研究轉譯而成。Built with AI-assisted development; content translated from psychology research into accessible copy.
學到什麼What I Learned
再次確認轉譯心理學概念時，精準且謹慎的用字非常重要——並清楚標示這是自我反思工具，而非診斷。Reinforced how important careful, accurate framing is when translating psychological concepts for a general audience — and clearly labeling it as self-reflection, not diagnosis.
對話式短影音生成工具Dialogue-to-Video Generator
發想Idea
將文字對話腳本更有效率地轉換為短影音內容的工具。A tool to turn written dialogue/scripts into short-form video content more efficiently.
使用者需求User Need
手動製作對話式短影音耗時且重複性高。Producing dialogue-style short videos manually is slow and repetitive.
體驗Experience
貼上腳本，即可產出結構化的短影音草稿。Paste a script in, get a structured short-video draft out.
AI 輔助Assistance
屬於 AI 輔助的創作流程實驗，而非完整軟體產品。An AI-assisted creative workflow experiment, not a full software product.
學到什麼What I Learned
實際動手做 AI 輔助流程實驗，讓我更清楚自動化真正能幫上忙的地方，以及仍然需要人的判斷的地方。Hands-on experimentation with AI-assisted workflows sharpened my sense of where automation genuinely helps content production — and where human judgment still matters most.

正在探索的方向What I'm Exploring

我正在探索科技與人的需求交會的可能，特別關注健康、Wellness、教育與人類發展。 I'm interested in opportunities where technology intersects with human needs — particularly across health, wellness, education and human development.

比起將自己限制在單一產業，我更想理解新興科技、數位產品與更好的體驗，如何幫助人們生活、學習，以及更理解自己。 Rather than following a single industry path, I'm interested in understanding how emerging technologies, digital products and better experiences can help people live, learn and understand themselves differently.

目前在尋找What I'm Looking For

目前希望尋找產品、行銷策略、成長或合作夥伴相關機會，加入以科技驅動的團隊，特別關注消費者需求與健康、Wellness、教育或人類發展交會的領域。 I'm looking for opportunities in product, marketing strategy, growth or partnerships within technology-driven teams, particularly where consumer needs intersect with health, wellness, education or human development.
