關於我About Me

過去十年間，我從 PChome 的電商營運起步，歷經偉門廣告的整合行銷、上海新創的品牌孵化，到丹麥家具集團的亞太品牌策略。每一段經歷都讓我學會，在不同的市場、團隊與資源條件下，找到品牌成長的方向，並把策略一步步落實成成果。 Over the past decade, I've built my career from e-commerce operations at PChome, through integrated marketing at Wunderman, brand incubation at a Shanghai startup, to Asia-Pacific brand strategy at a Danish furniture group. Each chapter taught me how to find the direction for brand growth — and turn strategy into measurable results — across different markets, teams, and resource constraints.

三年前回到台灣，我選擇投入創業，親自從零打造品牌，將品牌定位、產品開發、通路拓展到內容經營，放進真實市場中反覆驗證。這段經歷讓我不只是累積更多技能，也重新確認自己最熱衷於「參與品牌從無到有、從策略到落地的每一個關鍵階段」。 Three years ago I returned to Taiwan and chose to build from zero — taking brand positioning, product development, channel expansion, and content operations into the real market to test and iterate. This experience didn't just add to my skills; it confirmed what I'm most passionate about: being part of every critical stage as a brand goes from nothing to something real.

現在我希望將這十年累積的品牌策略、商業思維與跨域整合能力，帶到更大的平台，持續打造真正具有長期價值，也能建立健康生活的產業。 Now I'm ready to bring a decade of brand strategy, business thinking, and cross-domain integration into a larger platform — to keep building brands with lasting value in the wellness and lifestyle space.
